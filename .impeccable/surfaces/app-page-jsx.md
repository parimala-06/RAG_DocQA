## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole app reads as a single open workpaper, not a chat window — every claim on the page has already been tied out, or is waiting to be, refusing the generic sidebar-plus-bubbles chat shell this category always ships.

OWN-WORLD: Pale ledger-green paper (#EEF1EA) for the index rail, warm paper white (#FBFAF9) for the workpaper column, ink (#1A1D19) for all text, one accent — tie-out green (#2E6B4A) — carried only by tick-mark citations, active states, and confirmations; alert red (#B3261E) reserved for delete/error, never decoration. IBM Plex Sans for reading text, IBM Plex Mono with tabular figures for every number, index code, and citation tick. Thin horizontal ledger rules throughout; every control keeps a visible border; zero shadows, zero border-radius except the tick-mark glyph itself.

STORY: A recruiter opens a trial-balance-style index of the sample documents, uploads or picks one, watches a live tabular readout tie it out (chunked, embedded, indexed), asks a question, and reads an answer where every claim carries a hand tick mark that flips open its exact supporting excerpt — trust made legible as a bookkeeping ritual, not an assertion.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail (pale ledger green, ~20rem, ruled rows): "TRIAL BALANCE" header over the document list, each row a workpaper index tab (W/P 1, W/P 2…) with filename, summarize/open/delete controls; upload control at the top styled as opening a new schedule line, dashed rule, mono placeholder. Right column (paper white): empty state reads as an unopened workpaper inviting the first upload; once a document exists, the question line sits pinned at the top of the column, bordered input, tabular placeholder copy, primary Ask button in ink with the tie-out-green hover.

FORM: Grounded candidate "The Tie-Out Ledger" (accountant's audit workpapers — tick marks that tie a figure to its supporting schedule), chosen directly by the user from the safer-register re-roll over "The Verification Desk," "The Reviewer's Margin," and the plain category standard. Seed key 3439a767.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
